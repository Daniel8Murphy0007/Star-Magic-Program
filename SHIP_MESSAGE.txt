- NGC 1275 (Perseus A), the Perseus-cluster BCG, is the only 29-doc
  system with BOTH an AGN jet-feedback force (F_BH) and a cold
  filamentary-gas term (M_fil).
- F_BH = P_jet/r_jet AGN jet reaction force on the ICM; P_jet ~ 1e35 W
  (Chandra X-ray cavities, Fabian 2003), r_jet = 10 kpc = 3.086e20 m:
  F_BH = 3.24e14; normalized F_BH/rho_ICM = 3.24e14/3e-26 = 1.08e40
  m/s^2 -> AGN feedback vastly exceeds local gravity, preventing
  runaway cooling.
- Feedback-balance theorem: P_jet ~ L_X_cooling ~ 1e35 W (Chandra) ->
  self-regulated AGN feedback (the BH modulates jet power to match the
  cooling luminosity).
- M_fil cold filaments: ~100 optical Halpha filaments (Lynds 1970,
  Fabian 2008), total mass ~1e8 M_sun = 2e38 kg, +/-300 km/s, T
  1e4-1e5 K (vs 3e7 K ICM), up to 50 kpc; g_fil = G*M_fil/r^2 =
  1.40e-13 m/s^2 (~1000x below base gravity, aiding re-accretion).
- Perseus feedback cycle: filaments fall -> feed AGN -> P_jet up ->
  F_BH up -> heating up -> cooling slows.
