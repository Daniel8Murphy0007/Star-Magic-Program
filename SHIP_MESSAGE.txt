Physics:
- The Pillars of Creation (M16, NGC 6611, ~6500 ly) 9-term MUGE with a
  novel decaying photoevaporation erosion factor E(t) = E_0*e^-t/tau_e
  (E_0=0.1, tau_e=1 Myr) applied as a multiplicative suppression
  (1 - E(t)) on the base gravity.
- At t=0: E=0.1 -> 10% suppression (max erosion); at t>>tau_e: E->0 ->
  gravity recovers as pillar material disperses / EUV source weakens.
  (1-E(t)) = fractional mass remaining after EUV ablation.
- Sign taxonomy (erosion vs compression): Pillars (1-E), E_0=0.1,
  NEGATIVE - EUV ablation removes mass -> less gravity; Bubble Nebula
  (1+E), POSITIVE - shock compression -> more gravity (PAPER_221);
  Orion - none (pre-dispersal). Sign set by whether the process removes
  or adds mass to the gravitating region.
- Canonical result at t=0.1 Myr (M=100 M_sun, r=5 ly=4.73e16 m, same
  M16 radius as PAPER_219): E=0.0905, (1-E)=0.9095;
  a_base = G*M/r^2*(1-E) = 5.933e-12*0.9095 = 5.40e-12 m/s^2. Verified.
- M_dot_factor = M_gas/M_init = 10000/100 = 100. Doc 7 of the
  grok_share_8d951e12 thread.
