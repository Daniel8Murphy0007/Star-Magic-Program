\begin{abstract}
We close ten Standard-Model particle-physics observables from the eleven
frozen UQFF primitives: the proton/electron, muon/electron, tauon/electron,
and tauon/muon mass ratios; the Higgs, $W/Z$, neutron/proton ratios; the
proton and electron rest masses in MeV; and the fine-structure constant
$\alpha$. \textbf{All ten closures land below 0.15\%}; six are below
0.05\%, and the proton rest mass closes to within $\mathbf{0.0024\%}$:
$m_p = \Nch\cdot\SOfive^2+\Nch\Dphys+\KMex+2\Ftrz\Phires = 938.25$~MeV.
The proton/electron ratio admits the clean integer-coefficient form
$m_p/m_e = \Afive(\Dcrit+\Dphys)+\Nch\Dphys = 1836$, matching the
CODATA value $1836.15$ to $0.008\%$. Cumulative total: \textbf{257 closures}.
\end{abstract}

$m_\tau/m_e \approx \Afive^{2}+(\text{corrections})$ at $0.011\%$.

$\Afive$ scaling.

CODATA value. The closure isolates $\alpha$ at the TRZ-cubed scale with
a TRZ-quartic correction, exhibiting the characteristic small-scale
hierarchy of the electromagnetic coupling.
