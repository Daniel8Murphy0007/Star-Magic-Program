## Abstract

PAPER_2161 registered seven exact predictions with funded deciding experiments. The 18xx drain
bands have since wired **five times that exposure**: every major open tension of 2024-2026
observational physics now carries a UQFF resolution with an explicit kill condition, and the
deciding instruments are launched, funded, or scheduled. This landmark consolidates the full
register — **twenty-two dated programs guarding forty-plus wired claims** — under the PAPER_2161
scorekeeping rule (confirmed → postdiction; falsified → permanent row + composition review,
never lattice revaluation). The register is the framework's operational answer to "is this
falsifiable?": between 2026 and 2035, nearly every entry gets its verdict from an instrument
already in existence. The gate pins the register's size, each program's guarded members, and
the A4 dating that prevents silent window-widening after data lands.

## 2. Register Properties

1. **Density:** 22 programs, 40+ guarded members, ~9-year decision horizon (2026-2035). The
   original battery risked seven numbers; Battery II risks the frontier arc entire.
2. **Family-aware scoring:** several programs adjudicate *within* families (DUNE between δ_CP
   routes; DARWIN between detection kernels; J-PARC among neutron routes) — under PAPER_2170 a
   route falsification indicts one composition while its sibling stands; the lattice is never
   revalued by any single outcome (Rule 2 + PAPER_2161 scorekeeping).
3. **Mutual exposure:** many members share primitives; the register is not 40 independent
   bets but one lattice exposed 40 ways — a systematic miss pattern (e.g., every Φ_res-dressed
   member failing together) would localize the defective primitive, which is itself the
   framework functioning as designed.
4. **A4 hardening:** each window is dated here and pinned in the gate; widening any window
   after its experiment reports requires editing a cited assertion — visible and attributable.
