## Methodology

Corpus-wide regex sweep of whitepapers/*.md for compound-prefix signature patterns:
- `primitive × (1+F_TRZ)` — simple additive-F_TRZ perturbation
- `primitive × (1-F_TRZ)` — simple complement-F_TRZ
- `primitive × (1±F_TRZ)²` — squared F_TRZ perturbation
- `primitive × (D_phys+F_TRZ)` — integer-shift additive form
- `primitive × (n+F_TRZ)` — higher-shift forms

Each match extracted with 250-char context and classified by:
- Sub-family type (simple/complement/squared/additive/higher-shift/multi-primitive)
- Physical regime
- Primary primitive components
