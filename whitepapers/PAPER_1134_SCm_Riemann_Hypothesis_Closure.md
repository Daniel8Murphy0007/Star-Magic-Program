## 1. The Riemann Hypothesis

The Riemann zeta function for $\mathrm{Re}(s) > 1$:

$$\zeta(s) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^s} = \prod_{p\ \mathrm{prime}} \frac{1}{1 - p^{-s}}$$

analytically continued to all $s \in \mathbb{C}$. The non-trivial zeros $\rho_n$
satisfy:

$$\zeta(\rho_n) = 0 \qquad \mathrm{Re}(\rho_n) \in (0, 1)$$

The **Riemann Hypothesis** (RH): $\mathrm{Re}(\rho_n) = \frac{1}{2}$ for all $n$.

All verified zeros lie on the critical line $\mathrm{Re}(s) = \frac{1}{2}$.
The first 50 imaginary parts $\gamma_n = \mathrm{Im}(\rho_n)$ are tabulated by
Odlyzko and are used in this paper.
