## Section 1: Gap Structure

The energy gap between states $|a\rangle$ and $|b\rangle$ is defined as:

$$\Delta E_{ab} = E_b - E_a$$

In the UQFF framework, these gaps depend on field configuration parameters.

## Section 2: Closure Mechanisms

When the driving frequency matches a natural frequency:
$$\omega_{drive} = \omega_{natural}$$

The gap can be resonantly driven closed.

### Adiabatic Evolution

Slow parameter changes allow adiabatic following:
$$\frac{d\lambda}{dt} \ll \frac{\Delta E}{\hbar}$$

This preserves quantum state during transitions.

### Quantum Tunneling

Non-resonant gap closure via tunneling:
$$\Gamma = \Gamma_0 e^{-S/\hbar}$$

where $S$ is the action.
