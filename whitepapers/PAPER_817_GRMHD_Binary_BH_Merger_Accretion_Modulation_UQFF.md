$$\dot{M}_{binary} \propto f_{orb} \cdot (1 + A \cdot \cos(\omega t + \phi))$$

- $\omega = 2\pi f_{orb}$ = angular orbital frequency
- $\phi$ = phase offset

beat frequency between the inner disk and binary.
